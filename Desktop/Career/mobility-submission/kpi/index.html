How the baseline comparison works

The baseline table's grain is month × ISO weekday × hour × pickup area (8,064 slots, 4–5 observed dates each), computed over full-year 2025. To compare a week: take the week's 168 calendar hours, join each to its slot, and sum.

  • Baseline volume = Σ avg_trip_count across the week's slots (the historical baseline for that calendar week).
  • Envelope = Σ p25 and p75 separately — the baseline band of variation.
  • Population: every figure on this board — KPIs, charts, tables, and the baseline — is computed on all rows, no population filter.
  • Leakage caveat: the baseline includes dates after the Mar–Apr 2025 window, so it is a seasonal reference, not an as-of forecast. Use: "is this week unusual for its calendar week?", not "did we beat forecast".
